Sitemap Crawl Audit

Pull every URL from a sitemap and record its robots directive — index/noindex, follow/nofollow — into a spreadsheet.

    Every URL costs one request. Turn this off for a fast URL-only export.

    Rows past this are marked Not checked. Lower it if the download times out.

    Hosting caps how long one request may run, so large sitemaps can’t be fully checked here. For a complete audit run scripts/add_robots_column.py on your own machine.
    Index, follow Noindex or nofollow Unreachable / not checked
